Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Who we are: LNJK Studios ("we", "us"), the makers of Roadimentary (the "Game").
Contact: team.lnjkstudio@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Game and this website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and try to meet common international standards (such as the GDPR) as well.

1. What we collect

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

2. Who we share it with

We only share information with services we need to run the Game:

  • Microsoft PlayFab – accounts, login, saved progress, profiles, friends and messages.
  • Photon (Exit Games) – online multiplayer, match chat and voice.
  • PayMongo – payment processing for in-game purchases.
  • Other players – your username, avatar, bio, likes and online status are visible to others. Messages are visible only to the friend you send them to.
  • Authorities – only if the law requires it.

These providers may store data on servers outside the Philippines. They are required to protect it.

3. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except purchase records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

4. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct wrong information;
  • object to or withdraw consent for processing;
  • ask us to delete your account and data;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To do any of these, email team.lnjkstudio@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 15 days.

5. Deleting your account

Email team.lnjkstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address you registered with. We will confirm, then delete your account, progress, profile, friends and messages. Deletion cannot be undone, and unused in-game currency is lost.

6. Children

The Game is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it. Players under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission.

7. Security

We use secure connections and trusted service providers, and we limit who can see your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the Game or on our website for important changes.
