Sample work order / Highway Run

Your First Repair Job

A stretch of highway needs repairs. Read the brief, plan around your budget, and get your beaver crew on site.

  • Damaged pavement
  • Job budget
  • Repair objectives

  1. Pick a contract

    Check what’s damaged and what the job asks of you. Weigh the conditions and payout before you sign on.

  2. Choose materials and crew

    Match your supplies and beaver crew to the job. Pick what the repair needs without spending the whole budget up front.

  3. Repair the road

    Work through the repair objectives on site. Clear damaged material, prepare the area, and finish the surface as the job requires.

  4. Review your results

    See how the job turned out, then carry your operator progress into the next contract. Use what worked to plan a better repair.
